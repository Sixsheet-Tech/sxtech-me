**Short answer:** start with one business outcome you care about this year — not with a tool or a platform. Pick the team that owns that outcome, find two or three workflows where AI can save time or improve quality, and run a 90-day plan with clear measures. Scale only what works.

Many leaders feel they are already late on AI. That pressure pushes companies into one of two traps: buying tools nobody uses, or launching a large programme that takes a year before anyone sees value.

## Trap 1: Starting with tools

Licences are easy to buy. Changing how people work is not. Without clear use cases and support, usage drops after the first month and AI becomes "something we tried".

## Trap 2: Starting too big

A company-wide AI strategy is useful, but if the first result is twelve months away, momentum dies. Leaders need early, visible wins to keep teams engaged.

## A better starting point: one outcome, 90 days

### 1. Choose one business outcome
Faster proposal turnaround, more content with the same team, quicker customer replies, less time on reporting. Make it something the business already measures.

### 2. Involve the people who own the work
The team doing the work knows where the friction is. Transformation happens when business and people change together — not when a plan is handed down.

### 3. Map the workflow and find two or three AI opportunities
Draw the current process end to end. Mark the repetitive steps: drafting, summarising, searching, formatting, answering the same questions.

### 4. Build capability, not just access
Train the team on their own use cases, with their own documents. Hands-on practice beats a generic AI course.

### 5. Measure, then scale
Compare time, quality and output before and after. Keep what works, drop what doesn't, then repeat with the next team.

| Phase | Weeks | What leaders should see |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | 1–2 | One agreed outcome and a map of the current workflow |
| Design | 3–4 | Two or three AI use cases with owners |
| Learn & build | 5–10 | Team trained, first workflows running |
| Adopt | 11–13 | Before/after results and a decision to scale |

## FAQ

### How should a company start AI transformation?
Start with one measurable business outcome and the team that owns it. Map their workflow, pick two or three AI use cases, train the team on real work and measure results within 90 days.

### Do we need a data team before using AI?
No. Many high-value use cases — drafting, summarising, research and content — work with the documents teams already have. Data foundations matter later, for deeper automation and analytics.

### What should executives measure in an AI pilot?
Measure what the business already tracks: time per task, turnaround, output volume, quality or error rates, and how many people are still using AI after the pilot.
